Our story

A little boutique with a big heart for Main Street.

Maude on Main

Our mission is simple: to provide quality apparel, footwear, accessories and gifts for any occasion. We strive to offer timeless pieces for every fit, and we love helping you find the one that feels like you.

You’ll find us in Historic Downtown Van Buren, Arkansas, where the racks change with the seasons and a friendly face is always waiting at the door.

Labels we carry include Gilli, Blowfish, Mono B, Hem & Thread, Kinsley Armelle, Haptics, Yellow Box, Ces Femme, Canvas Apparel and Doe & Rae.
